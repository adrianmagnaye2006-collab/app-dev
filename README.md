# My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021)

<img width="300" height="400" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1a9580df-c74c-49bd-af8a-16a43097bb4b" />

**Spider-Man: No Way Home** is a 2021 superhero film directed by Jon Watts. After Peter Parker's secret identity is exposed to the world, a spell gone wrong breaks open the *multiverse*, bringing dangerous villains from alternate realities into his world.

> "With great power, there must also come great responsibility."

## Why I Love It
* **Epic Crossovers:** Seeing three generations of Spider-Man team up on screen.
* **Emotional Depth:** Peter faces real loss and makes a selfless sacrifice to protect his friends.
* **Iconic Villains:** Classic enemies like Green Goblin and Doc Ock return with unforgettable performances.

## Top Favorite Characters
1. Peter Parker (Tom Holland)
2. Peter-Two (Tobey Maguire)
3. Peter-Three (Andrew Garfield)
4. Norman Osborn / Green Goblin

# My Favorite Anime: Dragon Ball Super 2 Season

<img width="300" height="400" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/61b1f0ce-e972-4344-ad4d-ceb32be4cc19" />


**Dragon Ball Super** is a Japanese anime and manga series created by Akira Toriyama and Toyotarou. Following the defeat of Majin Buu, Goku and Vegeta encounter *Gods of Destruction*, alternate universes, and divine transformations as they push past their mortal limits.

> "Power comes in response to a need, not a desire."

## Why I Love It
* **Epic Transformations:** Iconic forms like Ultra Instinct, Ultra Ego, and Gohan Beast.
* **Unmatched Action:** High-speed martial arts battles with universe-shaking stakes.
* **Character Growth:** Seeing former rivals like Vegeta and Piccolo become true protectors.

## Top Favorite Characters
1. Son Goku
2. Vegeta
3. Son Gohan
4. Piccolo
